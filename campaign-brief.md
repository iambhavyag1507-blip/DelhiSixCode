# Campaign Brief: Delhi Six Couture
**Date:** 2026-05-11
**Platforms:** Meta (Instagram + Facebook), Google Search
**Objective:** Awareness + Lead Generation (consultation bookings)

**Offer:** Free bespoke consultation — heritage craftsmanship angle

---

## Brand DNA Summary

Delhi Six Couture is a heritage-rooted bridal couture house whose visual and verbal identity draws from the artisanal legacy of Old Delhi — expressed through deep crimson and antique gold, Playfair Display headlines, and luxury editorial photography of embroidery, fabric, and brides in regal settings. The brand speaks with quiet emotional authority: intimate rather than loud, aspirational rather than transactional, honoring Indian craft and culture over mass-market trend. Its core audience — Indian brides aged 24–38 in the upper-middle to luxury segment — is not buying a lehenga; she is commissioning a one-of-a-kind heirloom that carries her story, and Delhi Six Couture positions itself as the only custodian worthy of that trust.

## Campaign Concepts

---

### Concept 1: "Woven in Old Delhi"
**Angle:** Heritage story / brand awareness

**Hypothesis:** Cold audiences on Meta have no frame of reference for Delhi Six Couture. Leading with the brand's Old Delhi origin story — its craftspeople, its lane, its generational knowledge — builds emotional permission before any product ask. Awareness-first sequencing: story now, consultation ask in retargeting.

**Primary Message:** Every stitch carries four hundred years of Old Delhi craftsmanship. Your lehenga should too.

**Tone:** Heritage-rooted, reverent, storytelling — like a letter from an artisan, not a retail ad.

**Visual Direction:** Close-up of an artisan's hands at a frame embroidering zardozi on deep crimson fabric. Warm candlelight tones. Soft focus background revealing a workshop interior with bobbins and thread. No model face — the craft is the hero. End frame: brand wordmark in Playfair Display on #f7efe6 cream with a single line of body copy.

**Target Platforms:** Meta (Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed)

**CTA:** "Discover Our Story" (awareness) → retargeting CTA shifts to "Book Your Free Consultation"

**Addresses:**
- Pain point: mass-produced designs without cultural authenticity
- Aspiration: celebrate Indian craft and heritage on their wedding day

**Andromeda Diversity Note (Meta):** Deploy two visual format variants to avoid audience saturation. Variant A — Instagram Feed (1080x1080): static editorial photograph of the artisan's hands, minimal copy overlay. Variant B — Instagram Reels/Stories (1080x1920): 15-second video reel showing zardozi in progress, needle catching candlelight, with a slow zoom-out revealing the full embroidered motif. The motivator shifts between formats: Variant A motivates through timeless beauty (aesthetic desire); Variant B motivates through craft process and exclusivity (behind-the-scenes intimacy). These distinct motivators reach different sub-segments within the same lookalike audience pool.

### Concept 2: "This Is Your Moment"
**Angle:** Bride as star / emotional aspiration

**Hypothesis:** Warm audiences (site visitors, video viewers, engaged followers) have already demonstrated interest. They need emotional permission to commit — a mirror held up that shows them wearing the thing they have been imagining. The Star-Story-Solution framework places the bride as the protagonist; the couture is simply what makes the story possible.

**Primary Message:** She imagined this day her whole life. She deserves a lehenga that was made only for her.

**Tone:** Intimate, emotionally warm, cinematic — a wedding film voiceover, not a product description.

**Visual Direction:** A bride in a richly embroidered deep crimson lehenga standing in a heritage haveli courtyard. Dappled morning light through a jali screen. She is not looking at the camera — she is in a private moment, slightly turning, fabric catching the light. The composition shows generous negative space above and to the left; the lehenga fills the right half of the frame with texture and detail. Color grading: warm amber and cream, no cool tones.

**Target Platforms:** Meta (Instagram Feed + Stories), Google Display

**CTA:** "Book Your Free Consultation" (primary lead CTA across all placements)

**Addresses:**
- Pain point: generic off-the-rack bridal wear with no personal story
- Aspiration: wear a one-of-a-kind ensemble that tells their love story; feel like royalty with perfectly fitted bespoke couture

**Star-Story-Solution Framework (Meta copy):**
- **Star:** She has been picturing this lehenga since she was a little girl.
- **Story:** But every boutique she visited showed her the same twelve designs — beautiful, yes, but worn by a hundred other brides before her.
- **Solution:** Delhi Six Couture creates one lehenga. For one bride. Rooted in four centuries of Old Delhi embroidery. Book your free consultation and begin yours.

**Andromeda Diversity Note (Meta):** Variant A — Instagram Feed (1080x1080): the haveli courtyard bride photograph with a short 3-line copy overlay. The motivator is aspiration and self-identity ("she deserves"). Variant B — Instagram Stories (1080x1920): a full-screen sequential carousel of 3 cards — Card 1: close-up of the bride's embroidered dupatta detail; Card 2: wider shot revealing the full bridal look; Card 3: CTA card on cream background with consultation offer. The motivator shifts to urgency and exclusivity ("only X consultation slots available this season"). Different format, different motivator — reaches distinct emotional response profiles within the same warm retargeting pool.

### Concept 3: "The Search Ends Here"
**Angle:** High-intent search capture / lead generation

**Hypothesis:** Brides and families actively searching for bespoke bridal lehenga, couture bridal wear Delhi, or wedding lehenga designer are bottom-of-funnel. They have intent but have not yet chosen a brand. Google Search ads with AIDA-structured copy and a zero-friction consultation CTA capture this intent before a competitor does. This concept is conversion-first with no awareness preamble.

**Primary Message:** Custom-made, heritage-crafted bridal lehenga — your consultation is free. Book now.

**Tone:** Direct, confident, benefit-forward — answers the search query, signals authority, removes friction immediately.

**Target Platforms:** Google Search (primary), Google Display (retargeting)

**CTA:** "Book Free Bridal Consultation" (Google Search CTA extension), "Get Your Custom Lehenga — Free Consult" (Google Display)

**Addresses:**
- Pain point: difficulty finding couture that honors Indian heritage
- Aspiration: feel like royalty with perfectly fitted bespoke couture

**AIDA Framework (Google Search copy):**
- **Attention (Headline 1):** Bespoke Bridal Lehenga Delhi
- **Attention (Headline 2):** Custom Couture — Free Consultation
- **Attention (Headline 3):** Heritage Embroidery Since Old Delhi
- **Interest (Description 1):** Every Delhi Six Couture lehenga is designed for one bride — hand-embroidered with zardozi and gota by master artisans from Old Delhi's couture lane.
- **Desire (Description 2):** Limited consultations available this season. Tell us your vision and we will craft a lehenga that is entirely, irreversibly yours.
- **Action (Sitelink extensions):** Book Free Consultation | View Collections | Our Craft Story | Contact Atelier

**Target keywords (suggested match types):**
- "bespoke bridal lehenga" [Phrase]
- "custom wedding lehenga designer" [Phrase]

1. **Run `/ads audit`** — Before activating spend, run a full platform audit to establish baseline performance benchmarks, validate audience sizing, and identify any existing creative fatigue on Meta or Quality Score issues on Google Search.

3. **Build and QA campaigns** — Implement the three concepts as distinct campaigns (or campaign objectives) in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads. Apply the keyword list from Concept 3 with the recommended match types. Set up UTM parameters aligned by concept name for clean attribution.

After 14 days, suppress the weakest concept and reallocate budget to the top two. Re-run `/ads audit` at 30 days to assess CPL trends, consultation booking rate, and creative saturation signals.
